Handkerchief

Notes on every topic in mathematics

A long-form set of mathematical notes aiming to cover essentially everything — undergraduate through graduate material, and further afield into areas like type theory — written by Pratyay Sen and Anubhab Santra, structured in the spirit of Evan Chen's Napkin, one self-contained chapter at a time.
